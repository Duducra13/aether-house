About Us

Architecture
for the senses.

We design spaces that resonate — where material, light, and proportion create lasting emotional experiences.

Our Philosophy

We believe architecture should be felt before it is understood.

AETHER HOUSE was born from a conviction: that the built environment has the power to shape how people feel, move, and connect. Every project begins not with a form, but with an emotion we want to evoke.

Our practice bridges the precision of structural engineering with the intuition of artistic direction. We study the way light falls across a room at different hours, how footsteps echo on stone versus wood, how a corridor can slow the breath or open the chest.

This is architecture that doesn't just shelter — it communicates. Every joint, every surface, every threshold is a deliberate decision in service of the people who inhabit the space.

What Drives Us

Four principles that shape every decision.

01

Precision

Every dimension, every joint, every material choice is intentional. We obsess over millimetres because we know they compound into atmosphere.

02

Empathy

We design for the body first. How does the light feel at breakfast? Where does the eye rest? Architecture is lived, not just seen.

03

Structure

Beauty without rigour is decoration. We build on logic — structural honesty, material truth, and spatial clarity form the backbone of every project.

04

Resonance

A space should echo in memory long after you leave it. We design for that lasting imprint — the feeling you carry with you.

Our Journey

Key moments that defined us.

2013

Studio Founded

AETHER HOUSE opens its first atelier in Lisbon with a team of three architects and a shared conviction: that architecture must speak to the body, not just the eye.

2017

First International Project

Commissioned to redesign a private villa in the Algarve, the studio delivers its first project outside Lisbon — earning recognition for its sensory-driven approach to residential design.

2021

European Design Award

The Porto Cultural Centre project receives the European Design Award in the Architecture category, validating years of research into material-light interaction.

2025

Global Expansion

With projects spanning nine countries and a growing team of specialists, AETHER HOUSE establishes itself as a reference in sensory-driven architecture across Europe and beyond.
